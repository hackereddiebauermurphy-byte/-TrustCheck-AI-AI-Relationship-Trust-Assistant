‎💚 TrustCheck AI — Multi-Platform Relationship Trust Assistant
‎
‎Smart AI. Better Communication. Stronger Relationships.
‎
‎🚀 About the Project
‎
‎TrustCheck AI is a proposed AI-powered relationship assistant designed to help individuals and couples better understand communication patterns, address trust concerns, and build healthier relationships.
‎
‎The vision is to create a flexible, cross-platform solution that supports conversations and relationship guidance across popular social media and messaging platforms.
‎
‎🌐 Multi-Platform Compatibility
‎
‎The project aims to support users across a variety of popular platforms, including:
‎
‎- 💚 WhatsApp
‎- 🔵 Facebook and Facebook Messenger
‎- 📸 Instagram
‎- 🎵 TikTok
‎- 💬 Other compatible messaging and social media applications
‎
‎💻 Supported Devices
‎
‎The planned application is designed with cross-device compatibility in mind:
‎
‎- 📱 Android phones
‎- 🍎 iPhones
‎- 💻 Windows laptops and computers
‎- 🖥️ macOS computers
‎- 🌐 Compatible web browsers
‎
‎Compatibility will depend on the features and access permissions available for each platform. This project will not secretly access private accounts or bypass security protections.
‎
‎🤖 Key AI Features
‎
‎- AI Relationship Assistant: Guidance on communication, trust, boundaries, and relationship concerns.
‎- Consent-Based Conversation Analysis: Users can submit conversations they are authorized to share for AI-assisted analysis.
‎- Communication Pattern Insights: Help users reflect on misunderstandings, communication habits, and potential concerns.
‎- Multi-Platform Design: A unified experience designed for phones, tablets, laptops, and desktop computers.
‎- Privacy-First Approach: No unauthorized access to private messages, passwords, verification codes, or personal accounts.
‎- Relationship Guidance: Suggestions for honest conversations and healthier ways to resolve relationship difficulties.
‎
‎🎯 Project Vision
‎
‎Our vision is to explore how responsible AI can support relationship communication across multiple social platforms and devices.
‎
‎TrustCheck AI does not claim to prove cheating or determine someone's faithfulness through AI alone. Its purpose is to provide helpful insights while respecting privacy, consent, and personal boundaries.
